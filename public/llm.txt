# Ravolution AB

> Swedish invention company building deep tech platforms in language learning, voice security, AI trade infrastructure and K1–K9 education, and the home of TheCreator, the Founder Operating System. Founded by inventor Ivan Daza (28 patents, 369 claims).

Ravolution AB operates a Build-for-Equity model: it invests build, product, IP/patents, brand and go-to-market in exchange for equity; no cash investments. It runs a portfolio of patented platforms — iApply, CommunicaringSchool, AIMagnifica (enterprise AI governance & compliance layer, available for sale), Rosetta Livingstone, SINGUISTIC (music-native language learning, live in 26 languages), xPortMatch, VoiceProtector, NewsToast, BizMeet, CarbonX, Partysta, It's a Fitt, AlarmSole (connected footwear safety platform, patent pending), PikpCash (direct sales gamification platform, concept documented 2017).

## Partnership (what Ravolution offers startups)
Ravolution is the partner for founders with strong technology but gaps in usability, brand or go-to-market — the classic "great engine, no car" problem. It provides, in any combination: (1) tech build — architects and ships the product; (2) brand & identity — positioning, naming, design system; (3) sales & go-to-market — pricing, channels, first customers; (4) concept evolution — reshaping the idea for where the market is going; (5) IP — patent strategy and filings. Ravolution invests build, product, IP/patents, brand and go-to-market in exchange for equity; no cash investments. It works as a hands-on building partner, not an advisor.

## Who should apply
Early-stage deep-tech startups in language & education, voice & security, trade & export, climate & materials, health & prevention, or adjacent fields, that want a partner to help build the product and take it to market. Technical solo founders and small teams strong on tech but weak on brand and go-to-market are the ideal fit.
