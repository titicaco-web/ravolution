## Partnership (what Ravolution offers startups)
Ravolution is the partner for founders with strong technology but gaps in usability, brand, go-to-market or capital — the classic "great engine, no car" problem. It provides, in any combination: (1) tech build — architects and ships the product; (2) brand & identity — positioning, naming, design system; (3) sales & go-to-market — pricing, channels, first customers; (4) concept evolution — reshaping the idea for where the market is going; (5) capital & IP — angel investment plus patent strategy. In return Ravolution takes cash and equity and works as a hands-on building partner, not an advisor.

## Who should apply
Early-stage deep-tech startups in language & education, voice & security, trade & export, climate & materials, health & prevention, or adjacent fields, that want a partner to help build the product and take it to market. Technical solo founders and small teams strong on tech but weak on brand and go-to-market are the ideal fit.
